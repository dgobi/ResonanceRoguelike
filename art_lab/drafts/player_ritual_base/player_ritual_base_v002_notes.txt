PLAYER RITUAL BASE — v002 / right-facing traveler

Fresh native 32x32 drawing; v001 is preserved byte-for-byte as an earlier
frontal experiment. The new candidate is 28 pixels tall (y=2..29), with a
right-profile forehead, recessed eye, short nose and chin; open shoulders;
a filled chest tapering into a four-pixel waist; and two separate legs.
Both feet point screen-right and meet the existing y=29 baseline.

HAND POSITIONS (zero-based, inclusive canvas coordinates)

  Column 11 stays transparent beside the lower arm and hand.
- Screen-right forward hand: x=24..25, y=13..15, ahead of the lower chest/waist.
  A small upward thumb separates it from the horizontal forearm. Its center
  (24,14) is a useful provisional held-item anchor. The base contains no item.
These labels describe screen placement, not anatomical handedness.

CHARACTER AND COSTUME
Temporary petrol/teal clothing, warm skin planes and a restrained copper accent.
A short stepped collar has one inward-turning copper fold. The fitted tunic
continues into a short asymmetric split cloth; simple foot wraps suggest travel.
The low wrapped brow and tiny dark eye make a severe humanoid profile. No jackal
head, staff, copied emblem, or reference background was imported.
The figure uses continuous two/three-pixel limb masses, with selective shadow
planes instead of dark borders around each segment. The rear knee bends slightly.
At 1x, the collar turn reads primarily as a copper patch; its inward geometry
is clearer at 2x. Hands group fingers into small palm/mitten shapes.
