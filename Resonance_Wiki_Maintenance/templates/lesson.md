# Lxx — A concrete outcome

Outcome: one visible behavior. Prerequisites: named previous skills, not an entire course.
Baseline: full reviewed revision and the source-map topic. Working directory and interpreter: explicit.

## Predict
State the initial fixture, definition → consumer → registration → appearance → ownership chain.
Ask for a prediction about one change and its lifetime before revealing the answer.

## Build and observe
Supply a complete runnable file/patch or label each partial excerpt. Give exact commands,
expected diff, normal input, positive result and reset/cleanup boundary.
Explain only the key implementation decisions. Identify controlled provisioning.

## Change a requirement
Supply a meaningful variation and observable acceptance criteria.

## Diagnose
Introduce a real fault; distinguish at least two plausible causes with evidence.

<details><summary>Hint 1 — concept</summary>
Explain the ownership or dispatch boundary.
</details>
<details><summary>Hint 2 — source</summary>
Name the exact file and symbol.
</details>
<details><summary>Hint 3 — partial step</summary>
Give the next concrete observation or edit.
</details>
<details><summary>Full worked answer</summary>
Include every integration change and expected check; explain alternatives.
</details>

## Make a different example
Give a need and acceptance criteria while leaving a consequential design decision open.

## Evidence and limits
Separate factory, handler, input-loop, world construction, cold load and human learning.
Link final input fingerprints/receipt; explain fresh-instance/scene/world boundaries.
