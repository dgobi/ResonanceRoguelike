# L13 — Author an original expedition

Combine a place, threat, clue, useful reward and later consequence into one small playable idea. This capstone commissions a teaching scenario, not the pending production action-adventure package. Your fiction and aesthetic are yours to decide.

## Supplied checkpoint and prediction

Everything is independently supplied: encounter room, watcher, note, food chain, cottage/pool examples and survey mechanism. You can take the place route without completing every earlier content lesson. Understand the seven investigation questions and choose which system owns each decision.

```powershell
& $ResonancePython -X utf8 -B Resonance_Wiki_Maintenance/practice.py L13 --output evidence/guide-1/l13
```

The complete representative solution reads the clue, approaches and retreats from the guard, leaves/reenters an owned depleted scene, prepares and eats the exact crafted meal and records a later survey result that survives a checkpoint/cold load. Supplied materials/perk points are explicitly staged by the driver where needed. This verifies the controlled content graph; L07 separately verifies natural enrollment. Do not call the capstone an ordinary unassisted campaign expedition.

## Turn intent into a content graph

Write five sentences before editing: what the player notices; what they can infer; what threatens them; why the reward is useful; what remains different afterwards. Then map them to real definitions and consumers:

| Intent | Supplied implementation | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Notice a useful place | Room geometry, fire and visible exit | Construction/access and real render |
| Infer a safer response | `guide1_note` on readable carrier | Ordinary acquisition and reader |
| Choose an approach | Territorial watcher and traversable layout | Actual AI opportunities and return route |
| Gain something useful | Raw ingredient → passive meal → food use | Commitment, completion and nutrition route |
| Leave a later consequence | Depleted note/stock and survey completed count | Scene retention and cold state |

The full worked files are the L03–L07/L12 examples, installed together by the fixed launcher. The full executable integration is `lesson_runtime.py:expedition`, embedded in the solution reference. Reuse is deliberate: the new lesson decision is how the systems support an authored experience, not how to reconstruct every dependency from memory.

## Make your own bounded version

Copy `guide1_encounter.json` outside scans, sketch two approaches and move one clue/reward relationship. Choose a source-supported enemy recipe and resource need. Save dependencies in order: text/items/creature/access/pool, then room, then assignment or building membership if needed. Validate each edge, then play the complete route using ordinary input. Adapt the fixed route when coordinates change; a test must follow the intended experience without teleporting past its challenge.

**Guided variation:** make the clue warn about a safe retreat path and move the reward to require passing the watcher. Keep a second reachable route back. The complete variation is the same content graph with changed prose and coordinates; it is valid only if actual sight/geometry/AI make its claim true.

**Diagnosis:** all component probes pass, but the final reward is useless because its recipe output is never acquired or the place provides no stationary fire. Trace the player journey edge by edge; do not add another unrelated feature to compensate. A creature's name or a clue's prose cannot supply an unimplemented gate.

**Independent brief:** create a different small expedition with one meaningful tradeoff and one persisted consequence. Choose the fiction, spatial relationship, resource value and persistence owner yourself. Acceptance: complete dependencies, reachable ordinary inputs, deliberate appearance route, success/rejection/cancellation evidence where relevant, useful output, return path, saved depletion/consequence and an explanation of one design decision. Reference lookup is encouraged.
