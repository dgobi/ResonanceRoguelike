# L05 — Compose a creature with an understandable role

Create a territorial watcher that protects a place instead of simply increasing enemy damage. The fixture supplies room, light, player and actor; its fiction is deliberately neutral. Learn how a host, recipe, perception and saved commitment fit together.

## Start and predict

The independently supplied `guide1_watcher` derives from the current ordinary Barrow Watcher, retaining a validated territorial profile and explicitly local boundaries. It has 24 HP and a modest attack for practice. Predict whether renaming its ID copies every legacy power, whether `spawn_zones` places it, and whether a saved actor adopts a later recipe edit.

& $ResonancePython -X utf8 -B Resonance_Wiki_Maintenance/practice.py L05 --output evidence/guide-1/l05

The actual room constructor creates the actor. The player walks into its defended ground, becomes its observed target and retreats beyond its excursion boundary. The receipt checks its return to guarding, records before/engaged/after state and paid opportunities, then restores the scene in a fresh process. This demonstrates this guard scenario, not every AI behavior or whole-game balance. Inspect the real capture and log along with the state.

## Choose a supported behavior before tuning numbers

Use a permitted ordinary source in Workbench's Creature editor, then **Save as new variant**. Set the new ID/name, physical stats and perception; choose a supported composite recipe/entitled attachments. A recipe/first attachment change can require a new ID. Explicit local restrictions are part of the host contract. Validate before saving the room that references it.

The complete fixed solution is embedded here. It retains supplied art and harvest assets so you need not author those first.

## Vary and diagnose

**Guided variation:** reduce `defended_radius` from 4 to 2 while keeping a compatible excursion radius. Copy the full creature JSON to `authoring/guide1/watcher.json`, retain fixture ID and run `L05 --draft` to inspect the captured profile. In manual play, compare approaching the post and withdrawing beyond its defended area. Explain the boundary as behavior, not just a number.

**Fault:** clone a creature whose special power is registered under its old exact ID. Factory creation may succeed while the power is absent. Search `creature_abilities.ABILITY_REGISTRY`, then distinguish that legacy route from supported composite attachments. Do not “repair” the clone by pretending every ID automatically inherits code.

**Independent task:** design a second enemy for the same small room with a different decision: defend a smaller area, retreat farther after contact, or use another admitted recipe on a new ordinary host. Choose which perception evidence it needs. Acceptance: valid factory/host binding, actual placement, observed paid opportunities, an explainable difference, and saved effective profile after a cold load.
