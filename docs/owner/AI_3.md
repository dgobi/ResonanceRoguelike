## Runtime, counterplay and ownership

The typed attack result keeps commitment, authoritative hit/damage/death and
application acceptance separate. The resolver executes once and the old boolean
projection remains commitment. Only positive damaging hits on surviving eligible
recipients accept a fresh dose. Miss/dodge, zero damage, invincibility, lethal
hit, unsupported recipient, immunity, active dose, recovery and source suppression
cannot fabricate acceptance or renew withdrawal. Cornered defensive delivery
does not restart an exhausted retreat. Disruption keeps its existing action
policy; suppression disables delivery while leaving ordinary melee available.

The victim owns an optional version-1 record with copied timing/recovery policy,
stage, remaining counter, application ID, source creature/individual/scene and
applied/last-aged phase. Only the completed charged-phase lifecycle ages it,
including live retirement before serialization. Delivery in T spends no warning
at end T. Default end T+1/T+2 leaves warning 2/1; T+3 starts paralysis 2; T+4
leaves 1; T+5 restores movement with recovery 4; T+6 through T+9 spends recovery.
Transitions never spend the next stage. Multiple sources cannot refresh any
stage. Source death, suppression, departure or disappearance does not cure it.
Death terminates the condition and later events; remains do not inherit it.

The pure mobility query guards actual local/sprint/Press/chunk/stair and ordinary
legacy/composite/observation movement paths. It does not prohibit stationary
melee, door interaction, paid Wait or self-treatment. Blocked player moves are
free and change no position, credit, sprint or ownership state. Known venom
denial spends an ordinary AI wait without spatial failure or budget loss.
Real hold opportunities, evidence expiry, material geometry and scene policy
continue. Recovery grants movement permission without tactical replenishment.

Active, live and pending victims age once by identity. Pending arrivals can wait
at delay zero without losing destination/ownership. Source-scene extraction is
bookkeeping; same-scene promotion preserves a legal recorded position without an
unconditional inward nudge. Existing collision-repair semantics remain. Cached
creatures freeze remaining counters and resume without away-time catch-up.
No general offscreen simulation was added. Recipients are the living player and
ordinary non-special WanderingAI/CompositeAI actors with fighter/perception;
other special/NPC/AI recipients and allied treatment are unsupported.

Owned player treatment during warning or paralysis works at full HP, heals no
HP, clears impairment immediately, spends one selected unit and pays one phase.
That phase spends recovery's first count: default 4 leaves 3. No impairment,
existing recovery, death or stale item rejects without item/time cost or refresh.
Saved stack counts consume one unit while other grouped instances remain owned.

Application/progress/paralysis/treatment/recovery feedback and health conditions
describe mobility truthfully. Visible creature symptoms respect visibility;
unseen victims generate no narration. The bounded privileged inspector adds
delivery reasons, susceptibility, dose parameters/stage/clocks, identities and
movement denial without rerunning combat or aging. Sources consume only their
immediate delivery result and receive no hidden treatment notification.

## Compatibility and actual-play evidence

Absent optional extension state clears stale memory and means susceptible,
even if today's definition is immune. Nondefault saved immunity persists.
Saved sources retain their effective profile; fresh sources use new tuning.
Accepted doses retain their snapshot after definition edits or loss, including
the recovery used by a later cure. Owned remedies keep their saved use binding.
Malformed extension versions/stages/counters/provenance and incompatible saved
recipients reject through safe load. Neutral additions remain absent, preserving
historical serialization fingerprints and supported legacy/v1/v2 saves.

The final headless witnesses use saved rooms, room seed 20261027, combat RNG 17
and AI RNG 19, normal loadout/damage/AI/visibility and ordinary handler inputs.
They never inject a condition or an owned remedy. Actual pickup precedes legal
damaging delivery, acceptance and earned retreat. Three warning inputs precede
restriction for Nettle; six for Drowsing. Primary comparisons treat during warning,
treat during paralysis, wait through both restricted phases, or move three tiles
during warning before waiting. Treatment spends exactly one item/phase and leaves
recovery 3. Rejected movement spends none. Waiting reaches recovery 4 naturally;
expiry restores susceptibility and a later real bite earns a different application
ID. The rival witness progresses from real delivery through warning, paralysis,
recovery and removal on an ordinary Wandering victim.

These are bounded tactical witnesses, not final balance or unaided discovery.
The play traces include subsequent return/reapplication; different response
timelines should not be interpreted as a controlled damage-balance comparison.
Separate focused fixtures cover edge outcomes, full-HP treatment, multiple
injectors/speeds, costly multi-phase actions, stationary attacks, helpers,
transfer ownership, cold freezing, exhausted budgets and saved/fresh tuning.

including source provenance and remedy inventory. The final portable native
