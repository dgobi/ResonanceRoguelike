## Runtime and persistence

Observation uses a pure current normal-player-vision calculation shared with FOV
publication, including terrain, current position, ambient/underground radius,
equipped/visible secondary light, weather and eligible wraith exposure. Publication
still owns aura refresh, explored/entity memory, lighting and overmap revelation.
Queries/inspection do not advance those responsibilities or acquire prey.

The explicit supernatural permission senses exposure and locally useful shadow.
It identifies no watcher/prey and refreshes no sight evidence. Ordinary eligible
sight supplies retained player/rival identity, owning scene, position, provenance
and inclusive deadline. Empty hostility can still hide, without attacking.
Anonymous sound remains anonymous and cannot earn acceleration.

Cover uses bounded legal routing (512 cells, 16 steps), including non-greedy
movement, occupancy, furnishings and terrain/corner rules. Commitment survives
intermediate invisibility; reaching cover holds the rest of the phase. Replanning
and own visibility changes cannot refill patience. Up to eight exposed entries
remain failed; repeated sight at the same prey position cannot reset approach.
Eight no-progress opportunities, eight failed entries or the approach budget
retire pursuit. External local exposure/geometry changes or newly usable blocked
cover can rearm; changed observed prey identity/position rearms approach. The
fixed context is reanchored only on changed sight evidence. Stationary cover or
cowering is a resolved fallback, not a constantly renewing chase.

[The direct scheduling receipt](../../evidence/ai-2/scheduling.json) keeps both
fast opportunities and all three steps unseen: two ordinary decisions grant

normal step despite changed exposure conditions. This separates the spent marker
from exposure cancellation. The existing accumulator still grants at most two
ordinary decisions. Successful
unseen pursuit movement may continue one adjacent step once per actor/charged
phase. No extra attack/interaction/decision is available. Every committed step
checks current observation and actual grass reaction, then life, mobility,

interrupts the burst; rejected contact is not commitment. These movers skip
duplicate end-phase reaction. Legacy timing and Stone Lurker remain separate.

Saved state preserves optional prey, cover, finite counters, failed entries,
context/rearm fingerprints and spent bonus/reaction/cover phase markers. Routes

effective tuning; fresh actors use the current definition. No-prey concealment

revisit preserves the physical actor. Stale flags cannot enter transfer routes.
