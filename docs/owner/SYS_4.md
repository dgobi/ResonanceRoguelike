# SYS-4 — Working ruins and alternative access

The finite package delivers a secured Bound Coffer and an optional one-cell
Lashed Timber Screen, sharing captured fastening profiles. Production remains
on `main`; no branches, worktrees, development copies, personal-save loads or
desktop control were used. SYS-5 and wiki work are outside this delivery.

## Delivered behavior

Use **E → adjacent visible host → Enter**. Esc is free. For the shipped
`pale_lashings` profile:

| Method | Paid cost | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Careful cutting | Equipped owned cutting 1; three separate actions; sound 2 each | Coffer release, or screen removal plus exactly one wood bundle |
| Quick force | One action; sound 8 | Release/removal without salvage |
| Dissolution | One whole unused captured Dissolution dose of strength >=0.4; one action; no work sound | Release/removal without salvage |

The tool is retained. Continue cutting is the first option after a step.
Withdrawal, waiting and scene/cache residence do not advance work. Force/wash
can finish partial cutting, with the finishing method deciding salvage.
Six- and twelve-contact doses have equal access eligibility at equal strength;
the chooser identifies each captured payload and spends the exact whole unit.
Existing bile coating and newly named ordinary dose variants use the same
capability path. Raw reagents, Binding and coated weapons do not substitute.

Every browse/transfer route respects security, even for an empty coffer. Release
exposes its original item instances; ordinary taking does not roll new contents.
The screen changes actual tile walkability/transparency and restores captured
ground. Player movement, ordinary/composite AI, sound, FOV and shared light use
that geometry. It is not a bump-openable door. Quiet/loud pulses originate at the
player's actual work position after a successful geometry change.

Final completion is transactional: failed output construction/placement or exact
dose consumption restores geometry, progress and owners without time or sound.
Scene/address/target/equipment/dose changes, full paralysis and invalid reach
(including sealed corners) reject stale work. F5/F9, independent cold loading
and hot/cold caches preserve the effective profile and result. A map manifest,
container owner marker and strict geometry/state validation reject missing or
contradictory modern records before installation. Genuine historical containers
retain ordinary behavior; a decor ID alone never retroactively binds one.

See [Working ruins](../../roguelike/docs/WORKING_RUINS.md) for exact profile
bounds, supported floors, controls and persistence limits. Existing missing
definition/dependency limits remain; no fallback creates resources or releases
unknown security state.

reject forged cross-family/nested publication and recovery. Room/building

and screens lacking an intact alternative route reject before publication.
